# CampusOne (UniSphere) - University Campus Management Portal

> Unified academic, attendance, leaves, hostel, fees, results, and administrative management prototype for university students and faculty.

---

## 🌟 Overview

CampusOne (UniSphere) is a modern, responsive academic management portal designed for university campuses. It brings together all core student workflows alongside administrative tools in a unified, accessible interface.

---

## 📋 Included Workflows & Features

### 🎓 Student Modules
* **Dashboard**: Timetable, attendance stats, assignment deadlines, notices, and quick action shortcuts.
* **Attendance**: Course-wise attendance percentages, attended/conducted counts, and defaulter threshold indicators (<75%).
* **Class Leave (CL)**: Apply for lecture leave with medical or academic justification, track approvals and faculty remarks.
* **Hostel Leave (Outpass)**: Request hostel gate passes with emergency contact details and automated pass generation.
* **Assignments**: View coursework, submit completion notes, and receive grades with teacher feedback.
* **Events**: Browse university symposiums, workshops, and hackathons with instant RSVP tracking.
* **Academics & Results**: View semester SGPA/CGPA progression and subject grade cards.
* **Fees & Ledger**: Transparent breakdown of tuition, hostel, and exam fees with payment history.
* **Admissions Desk**: Application status tracking and entrance rank details.
* **Central Library**: Search catalog books, view borrowed books, due dates, and request 14-day renewals.
* **Helpdesk**: Raise grievance tickets across campus facilities (Hostel, LAN, Exam Cell) with status updates.
* **Profile & Documents**: Personal records, advisor information, and document verification status.

### 🛡️ Faculty & Admin Modules
* **Admin Dashboard**: Daily teaching schedule, pending approval counters, and quick administrative shortcuts.
* **Take Attendance**: Interactive roster to record present/absent student records for conducted classes.
* **Assignments & Grading**: Create new course assignments and grade student submissions with feedback.
* **Courses & Timetable**: Faculty teaching allocation, class schedules, and lecture room mappings.
* **Students Directory**: Campus-wide student roster with branch, semester, CGPA, and attendance filters.
* **Staff Profile & Leave**: Faculty profile and casual/duty leave application.
* **Approvals Desk**: Review student class leaves and hostel outpasses with custom remarks.
* **Notice Board**: Issue urgent or general campus-wide circulars.

## 💻 Tech Stack & Design Features

* **Framework**: React 19 + TypeScript + Vite.
* **Styling**: Tailwind CSS with custom academic dark mode support.
* **Icons**: Lucide React.
* **Responsive Simulation**: Supports Fluid Responsive, Workstation Desktop (1360px), Tablet (820px), and Mobile (420px) viewport modes.
* **Information Density**: Comfortable and Compact information density toggles.
